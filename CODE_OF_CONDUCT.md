# Code of Conduct

We want GEDS Explorer to be a respectful, evidence-driven place to collaborate.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith while still questioning evidence and design claims;
- welcome different backgrounds and accessibility needs;
- focus criticism on the work, not the person;
- protect private, personal, and security-sensitive information; and
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of personal data or secrets, and disruptive activity intended to prevent others from contributing.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security-reporting channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), marked “Code of Conduct”. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain the concern.
